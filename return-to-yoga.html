How to return to yoga after time away.

You are not starting over. You are meeting your practice—and yourself—as you are today.

Yoga Chapters · November 2026

Time away from yoga can gather a surprising amount of guilt. We remember what felt easier before, compare today’s body with an earlier version, and delay returning until we feel “ready.”

But yoga does not keep score. The practice has no attendance sheet. It asks only for your attention now.

Returning is not proof that you failed. Returning is the practice.

Make the first practice deliberately small

Do less than you think you should. Ten quiet minutes can rebuild trust more effectively than an ambitious class that leaves you depleted. Choose a few familiar movements and let curiosity replace measurement.

Notice what has changed without turning change into judgment. Strength, balance, and flexibility move in seasons. Your awareness can deepen even when a pose looks different.

A gentle return

  1. Take five unhurried breaths in a comfortable seat.
  2. Move through cat and cow slowly for one minute.
  3. Rest in child’s pose or another shape that feels supportive.
  4. Stand and fold only as far as the breath stays easy.
  5. Finish lying down for two quiet minutes.

Build continuity, not intensity

Decide when the next small practice will happen before this one ends. Consistency grows when the next step is clear and light enough to keep.

You do not have to recover an old practice. You can begin a new relationship with movement—one shaped by the life you have now.
